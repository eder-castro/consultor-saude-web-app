---
name: Weight chart default period
description: User-required default period and optional weight chart views.
---

The weight chart must open on the last seven days. Day, month and year remain selectable alternatives, not the initial view.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the initial monthly view and requested one week as the default.

**How to apply:** Preserve the weekly default when reopening the chart. Keep the minimum vertical span of 5 kg and distinguish absent readings from measured values; do not create averages or zero weights for missing days.
